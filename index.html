I’m Khwezi, a Johannesburg-based South African with a BA in Communication and English and a 120-hour TEFL certificate. I bring a love of language, a calm presence and a big belief that people learn best when they feel safe to make mistakes.

My studies taught me how to make ideas clear and connect with an audience. Now I’m bringing those strengths into the classroom: one thoughtful explanation, one good question and one small win at a time.
